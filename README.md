# Tiruppur Textile Export Tariff-Shock & Market Diversification

> Business Analyst portfolio case study analysing export-market concentration, tariff-shock exposure and diversification scenarios for Tiruppur knitwear businesses.

## 🚀 Live Dashboard

**[Open the interactive dashboard](https://sanjay-arlo.github.io/tiruppur-textile-tariff-shock-diversification/)**

## Business question

**How exposed is the export base to any single market, and which alternative markets would most reduce that risk?**

## Dashboard

Explore market concentration, revenue-at-risk, order recovery, buyer mix and the gap between current and target market diversification.

## KPI logic

**Market concentration %** = revenue from top market / total export revenue × 100.

**Revenue-at-risk** = revenue from exposed market × scenario order-loss rate.

## Dataset

Synthetic/illustrative export-order data is used for portfolio demonstration. It is not official Tiruppur Exporters' Association or DGFT shipment data.

## Business Analyst focus

The case demonstrates scenario analysis, concentration risk, commercial impact modelling and recommendation prioritisation.

## Production upgrade

## Limitation
