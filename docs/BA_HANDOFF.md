# BA Handoff — Tiruppur Textile Tariff Shock & Diversification

## Objective
Quantify export-market concentration, tariff-shock exposure and diversification opportunities for Tiruppur knitwear businesses.

## Stakeholders
Exporters; commercial/sales teams; finance; sourcing; industry associations; trade-policy stakeholders.

## Core requirements
- Track order value and units by market/month.
- Quantify top-market concentration.
- Model revenue-at-risk under tariff scenarios.
- Compare diversification target vs current mix.
- Explain scenario assumptions and keep data illustrative.

## Decision rule
Prioritise diversification where a single market contributes excessive revenue exposure and alternative markets offer viable volume/margin potential.

## Acceptance criteria
Market mix and risk KPIs reconcile; scenario assumptions are visible; filters work; recommendations expose drivers; synthetic-data disclosure remains clear.

## Production controls
Verified shipment/order data, tariff event lineage, buyer-market mapping, margin data, scenario versioning and sensitivity analysis.
