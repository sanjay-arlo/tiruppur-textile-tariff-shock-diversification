# BA / DA Delivery Pipeline

## Business question
How exposed is the export order book to tariff shocks and market concentration?

## 01 — Excel
Clean monthly orders, standardise market/product fields, calculate margin and tariff exposure, and identify concentration outliers.

## 02 — SQL
Calculate order value, market concentration, tariff cost proxy, margin risk and diversification gaps with reproducible queries.

## 03 — Power BI
Build: Export Executive → Market Mix → Tariff & Margin → Diversification Scenario. Use market/product/month drill-through.

## 04 — Decision
Recommend target markets/products where diversification reduces commercial concentration without destroying margin.

## Acceptance criteria
Order-value totals reconcile; tariff and margin measures have clear definitions; synthetic portfolio data is disclosed.
